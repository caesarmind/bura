How to play Bura

Goal. Two players race to take 31 points of cards in a hand (61 in 5-card Bura). A hand won is an eye; the first to the match’s eyes wins it.

The deck. 3-card Bura plays 20 cards — ace 11, ten 10, king 4, queen 3, jack 2, in four suits: 120 points. 5-card Bura adds the 9, 8, 7 and 6, worth nothing: 36 cards. The ten ranks above the king.

The deal. Three cards each (five in 5-card Bura). The next card names trump and lies crosswise under the stock — it is drawn last. Before the first hand one player guesses the trump’s colour: right, they lead; wrong, they deal. After that the winner of a hand deals the next.

Leading and answering. Lead one card, or several of one suit. The other answers with as many: to take them, every led card must be beaten — by a higher card of its suit or by any trump (a trump only by a higher trump). Can’t, or won’t? Give as many cards face down; the leader takes them unseen. Whoever takes leads next, and both draw back up, the taker first.

Declaring. Right after you take cards you may declare that you hold 31 (61). Right — the hand is yours; wrong — your opponent wins it. Otherwise play on. Your own pile can’t be counted (cards given to you face down were never seen) — remember it. Your opponent’s you may tap to see.

When the cards run out the last taker’s pile is counted: 31 (61) or more wins, otherwise the other player does.

Combinations. Bura — a whole hand of trumps — wins the hand at once. Maliutka — a whole hand in one other suit — answers any lead out of turn: the led cards go back and the Maliutka becomes the lead. Royal Bura (Royal 3-card): three aces with the trump ace — wins the match.

Raising. On your turn you may raise the stake: Davi ×2, Se ×3, Chari ×4, Panji ×5, Shashi ×6. The other accepts, raises back, or folds and pays the stake before the raise. Raises alternate. When a player is one eye from the match, nobody raises (Crawford).
